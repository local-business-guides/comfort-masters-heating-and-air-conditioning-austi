How to get ready for furnace work

A furnace installation plan may involve the furnace itself, its connection to the home’s existing heating system, controls, and other items that fit the agreed scope. The details depend on the equipment and the home, so ask the contractor to identify what is included rather than assuming every job covers the same work.

Before a visit, write down the make or model if you can find it, along with any problems you’ve noticed. Keep the space around the furnace accessible and mention anything that could make entry or movement difficult. It’s also sensible to confirm:
- Whether existing connections or controls will be evaluated.
- What needs to be moved or cleared before work starts.
- What cleanup and follow-up information are part of the visit.

A common mistake is focusing only on the furnace and overlooking the rest of the setup. Ask how the proposed work relates to the existing system, and make sure you understand the answer before approving a job.
